## 2. Fastest Catch-Up Sources This Week (August 1–18)

You have **2.5 weeks before Season 2 launches.** Use this time to gear to ~280-290 so Season 2 content is immediately accessible. Prioritize **solo-friendly and repeatable** sources.

### Week 1–2.5 Catch-Up Path (Priority Order)

#### **1. Delves (Solo-Friendly, Repeatable)**
**Tier & Rewards:**
- **Tiers 1–6:** Adventurer gear (259–276 ilvl)
- **Tiers 7–8:** Veteran gear (276–289 ilvl)
- **Tier 11:** Hero gear (302–315 ilvl) — only viable if already ~290+

**Why:** Solo-queue, role-agnostic, repeatable, quick runs (10–15 min per tier). End-of-run cache scales with difficulty.

**Strategy:** Farm Tier 7-8 to hit ~280-289, then transition to dungeons/vault.

**Sources:**
- [Everything You Need to Know About 12.1/Season 2 — Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/everythithing-you-need-to-know-about-12-1-season-2-delves-lairs-prey-mythic-raid-pvp-all-release-dates-rewards-and-more/)

#### **2. Voidforge System (Guaranteed Targeted Drops)**
**Available Now:** Nebulous Voidcores (catch-up available for all past weeks)

**How it works:**
- Spend Nebulous Voidcores to guarantee loot from specific raid bosses/M+ dungeons/delves.
- Each Voidcore = one targeted drop.
- New Voidcores awarded weekly; **catch-up is available** for characters joining late.

**Catch-Up Benefit:** You can instantly buy Voidcores for every week you missed, then target high-value gear immediately.

**Example:** Pick 2–3 key pieces (gloves, chest, boots) and spend Voidcores to target them from Mythic 0 or Delves.

**Sources:**
- [Turboboost and Bonus Rolls in Midnight Season 1: The Voidforge — Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/turboboost-and-bonus-rolls-in-midnight-season-1-the-voidforge/)
- [Coming Back to WoW? Here Is How to Catch Up — Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/blizzard-new-returning-player-guide/)

#### **3. Crafted Gear (Spark of Radiance)**
**Current Crafting Currency:** Spark of Radiance (not Spark of Dreams; Season 1 equivalent)

**Availability:**
- 1 Spark per week from repeatable quests in the main hub.
- Random drops from **any content** if you fall behind the cap.

**Cost:** 2 Sparks = 1 piece of gear (1-handed weapons, armor). 4 Sparks = 2-handed weapons/two-slot items.

**Item Level (Season 1 baseline):** Base quality ~259, scales with crafter profession level.

**Strategy:** Grab your first Spark this week, save it for Season 2 (August 18+) to craft a starter piece immediately.

**Sources:**
- [Spark Crafting Guide for Midnight — Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/spark-crafting-guide)
- [World of Warcraft PvE Gearing Guide — Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/midnight-pve-gearing-guide)

#### **4. Renown/Faction Vendors (Quick Wins)**
**Item Level:** Champion 1/6 (~246 ilvl) — low but instant.

**How:** Complete faction renown quests. Each faction offers 1–2 armor pieces.

**Why:** Fast initial boost; fills gaps while farming Delves/Voidforge.

**Sources:**
- [Stop Wasting Your First Week in Midnight on the Wrong Activities — Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/stop-wasting-your-first-week-in-midnight-on-the-wrong-activities/)

#### **5. Heroic Dungeons (If You Have a Group)**
**Rewards:** Veteran gear (276–289 ilvl)

**Advantage:** Repeatable, faster than Delves if you have DPS queues sorted.

**Disadvantage:** Requires a group; queue times for DPS.

**Sources:**
- [Midnight Season 2 Overview — Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/midnight/season-2-overview-dungeons-raids-dates)

#### **6. Mythic 0 (Available Aug 11+ with Patch 12.1)**
**Rewards:** 292 ilvl base (Champion tier)

**Availability:** August 11 (patch day), but Mythic+ keys don't start until August 18.

**Advantage:** Jumpstart to M+-ready ilvl in one week.

**Disadvantage:** Weekly lockout; one run per boss per week per character.

**Strategy (Aug 11–18):** If patch drops early, run all 8 dungeons for 8 pieces of 292 ilvl gear. This alone gets you M+-ready.

**Sources:**
- [Everything You Need to Know About 12.1/Season 2 — Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/everythithing-you-need-to-know-about-12-1-season-2-delves-lairs-prey-mythic-raid-pvp-all-release-dates-rewards-and-more/)

### Right Now (Aug 1–11): Recommended Daily/Weekly To-Do

1. **Delve Tier 7–8** (20 min/day): Grab 1–2 pieces, work toward 280+ ilvl.
2. **Renown quests** (10 min/day): Fill 1–2 slots with vendor gear.
3. **Voidforge cores** (2–3 min/day): Log in, claim the weekly core. Pre-target key pieces from Mythic 0 bosses.
4. **Spark quest** (5 min): Grab your weekly Spark; save for Season 2.
5. **Prey Hunts** (optional, 15 min/day): Flexible outdoor PvE for renown + drops.

## 3. Upgrade Currencies & Priority

### Season 2 Mistcrest System

Season 2 replaces "Dawncrests" with **Mistcrests** (different name, same function).

| Crest Type | Source | Caps | Upgrades To |
|------------|--------|------|------------|
| **Champion Mistcrest** | M+2–3, Normal raids, Delves 7–8, Prey Hunts | 750/week | Champion gear → 302 ilvl |
| **Hero Mistcrest** | M+4–8, Heroic raids, Delves 11, Prey Hunts 8+ | 650/week | Hero gear → 315 ilvl |
| **Myth Mistcrest** | M+9+, Mythic raids | 250/week | Myth gear → 328 ilvl |

**Upgrade Cost (Season 2 – Verified):**
- Flat cost per piece: ~40–50 Mistcrests (varies by upgrade step, not scaling).
- Full track upgrade (1/6 → 6/6): ~150 Mistcrests per piece.

**Sources:**
- [Item Levels Increasing in Midnight Season 2 — Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/news/item-levels-increasing-in-midnight-season-2-382120)
- [Midnight Season 2 Raises WoW Gear to 337 — Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/news/item-level-of-loot-in-midnight-season-2/)

### Upgrade Priority for M+ Pushers

1. **Weapon** (1st priority): Highest impact on DPS; often hardest to replace.
2. **Trinkets** (2nd priority): Outlaw-specific passives are rare; prioritize BiS trinkets early.
3. **Chest/Legs** (3rd priority): Highest item slot count; upgrade both simultaneously if possible.
4. **Remaining slots** (4th priority): Fill gaps after above are 6/6.

**Crest Spending Order:**
- Week 1–2: Spend **Champion Mistcrests** on weapon + trinkets.
- Week 2–4: Spend **Hero Mistcrests** as you transition to M+4+ (cap upgrades as you go).
- Week 4+: Grind **Myth Mistcrests** only if you're pushing M+9+.

## 4. Weekly Vault Strategy for Fresh Character

### Great Vault Structure (Confirmed for Season 2)

The Great Vault has three rows. Complete **objectives** to unlock choices:

| Row | Objective | Reward Track |
|-----|-----------|--------------|
| **Raids** | Raid Finder (1 drop) / Normal (2) / Heroic (3) / Mythic (4) | Follows raid difficulty |
| **Dungeons** | M+2–4 (1 drop) / M+5–8 (2) / M+9+ (3) | Follows M+ tier |
| **World** | Weekly events, quests, activities (1–4 drops depending on content) | Scaling tier |

**Week 1 (Aug 18–25, Season 2 Launch):**
- **Raid Row:** 0/4 (skip; raid tier not relevant yet)
- **Dungeon Row:** Complete **M+2 or higher** → 1 vault drop (aim for M+4+ if you can)
- **World Row:** **Capped at Champion 3/6 (~297 ilvl)** this week only
- **Total:** 2 vault drops (Dungeon + World)

**Week 2 (Aug 25–Sep 1):**
- **Dungeon Row:** Complete **M+4** → 2 vault drops
- **World Row:** Upgraded cap to **Hero 1/6 (~306 ilvl)** this week onward
- **Total:** 3+ vault drops (possible Raid + Dungeon + World)

**Ongoing Strategy (Week 3+):**
- **Aim to complete all 3 rows** every week for maximum vault loot.
- Prioritize **Dungeon Row** (fastest to fill, most consistent M+ gear).
- Fill **World Row** last (quests/events, slower but mandatory).

## 6. Outlaw-Specific Catch-Up Notes

### Stat Priority (Verified for Patch 12.0.7)

**Priority Order:**
1. **Agility** – primary stat (on all gear)
2. **Versatility** – highest secondary (boosts DPS + survivability)
3. **Haste** – 20–25% recommended (reduces GCD under Adrenaline Rush)
4. **Critical Strike** – Between the Eyes scales with crit; fill remaining slots

**Stat Weights:** Versatility ≈ Haste > Crit (rough parity for filler slots)

**Sources:**
- [Outlaw Rogue PvE DPS Stat Priority — 12.0.7 — Icy Veins](https://www.icy-veins.com/wow/outlaw-rogue-pve-dps-stat-priority)
- [Outlaw Rogue Stat Priority — Midnight — Wowhead](https://www.wowhead.com/guide/classes/rogue/outlaw/stat-priority-pve-dps)

### Must-Have Catch-Up Trinkets

**BiS Passive Pair (Season 2 Confirmed):**

| Trinket | Source | Priority |
|---------|--------|----------|
| **Gaze of the Alnseer** | Mythic raid (Chimaerus, The Venomous Abyss) | **#1 – Top passive trinket** |
| **Umbral Plume** (Crit version) | Mythic raid (Belo'ren, The Venomous Abyss) | **#2 – Fallback if Gaze unavailable** |

**Early Catch-Up Alternatives (Before Mythic Raid):**
- **Solarflare Prism** (if Gaze is unavailable early)
- **Ranger-Captain's Iridescent Insignia** (if Umbral is unavailable early)

**Why Passive Pair?** Outlaw has no impactful on-use cooldowns, so two passive trinkets sustain your already-high sustained DPS better than on-use combinations.

**Acquisition Path:**
1. **Week 1–2 (Aug 18–25):** Use Voidforge Nebulous Cores to target Venomous Abyss drops → run Raid Finder 1 boss to unlock Gaze/Umbral at base ilvl.
2. **Week 2+ (Aug 25+):** Push Heroic/Mythic raids or spend Voidforges on Voidforge cores to upgrade trinket ilvl.
3. **Fallback:** If Mythic raid is unavailable, grab Solarflare Prism + Ranger-Captain's Insignia from dungeon loot or crafting.
